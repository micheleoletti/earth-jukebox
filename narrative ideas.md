**narrative**
accessibility and emotional connection
"hear our planet playing the soundtrack of our lives"

each data type is mapped to a specific musical property or instrument
- lightnights => percussions
- winds => woodwinds (duh)
- sea currents => strings
- air pollution => detune strings
- ...

the arrangment depends on the viewport
zoom out and you hear everything
zoom in and the orchestra shrinks smoothly and out of view instruments stop playing
