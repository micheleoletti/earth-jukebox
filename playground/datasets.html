The datasets

Click a title for the SVS page. File list for any item: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/api/<id> (PNG frame sequences, MP4 movies, and a *_cbar.png colourbar to map colour back to real values).

Caveats

  • Frame index is not always time. Many movies include camera moves, zooms, title cards and fades. Map frames to dates before sonifying (Perpetual Ocean 2 ships a separate dates layer for this).
  • Steps differ. Yearly, 10-day, daily and sub-daily data cannot be lined up frame for frame. Resample to a shared step (10 days for the 2021 to 2023 overlap) or let each layer move at its own rhythm.
  • Two items are stills. GEOS Aerosols (2025-08-19) and GEDI Forest Height have no time axis. Sonify them by scanning across space instead of time.
  • Unknown dates. Air Quality (GEOS-CF) and the Atmospheric River have no dates in their metadata, so they are not placed on the timeline.
  • Items marked unverified have a time step inferred, not stated in the SVS metadata.
