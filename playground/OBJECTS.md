# What every 3D object on the globe is made of

Legend: **Real** = drawn from measured or modelled data at its true place and time. **Derived** = real data, reduced or smoothed (method stated). **Illustrative** = a visual choice that is not data (stated in the story footer).

| Object | Stories | Source | Status | Where the data ends and the drawing begins |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fire flames | Canada smoke, Black Summer, Africa fire belt | NASA FIRMS VIIRS active fire detections | **Real**, merged | Every detection is counted. Nearby detections at the same time are merged into one flame at their FRP-weighted centre (Canada: 2 km and 12 h, 47,329 detections into 12,162 flames; Australia: 5 km and 1 day, 507,028 into 29,059; Africa: 193 km and a quarter month, 8.3 million into 22,123). Size = summed fire power, linear. Flicker phase is random. Timing: in the daily and hourly stories a flame is visible for a stated time after its detection (Canada 30 h, Australia 2.5 days); in the monthly Africa story every flame of a calendar month is shown for that whole month and blends into the next month in its last 15% (same rule as the monthly maps). Was "1 in 17 drawn" before. |
| Station pillars | Canada smoke (New York PM2.5), Black Summer (Sydney, Canberra), Po Valley (ground NO2), Tonga (pressure jump), Iberia (station afternoon temperature) | OpenAQ / AirNow, NSW and ACT government monitors, EEA, ERA5 at the gauges (model) | **Real** | Height linear in the station value; linear between consecutive measurements; gaps stay empty and get no label. Height scale is exaggerated (stated; Black Summer 1.8x taller than the default). Labels: a few per view, never on a station without data (the stations with the highest current values, or a fixed list in Ian, Tides and Iberia). Iberia: slimmer pillars, height from 20 deg C (the few readings below are a flat stub, stated), bigger labels with whole numbers in Simple. |
| Ghost pillars | Po Valley, Iberia (1991 to 2020 normal per station) | EEA, same stations, 2015 to 2019 | **Real** (normal) | Faint glass pillar behind each city pillar = the mean of 2015 to 2019 for the same calendar day, same height scale as the 2020 pillar. |
| Water columns + ripple rings | Tides, Ian (surge), Tonga (sea wobble) | NOAA CO-OPS tide gauges | **Real** | Ian: 0 = normal high tide (MHHW), a column grows only above it (linear), a gauge below MHHW shows a short dark stub and its label gives the negative level. Tides: each gauge scaled between its own lowest and highest reading of the month (heights not comparable between gauges, labels in metres from the gauge's monthly mean). Tonga: linear from the lowest to the highest swing, drawn 1.8x taller. Ring brightness follows the level. Linear between readings (6 min to 1 h); a gauge that stops (Naples during Ian) stays empty. |
| Coast ribbon | Tides | NOAA CO-OPS tide gauges | **Derived** | Glowing beads on straight lines between neighbouring gauges of the same coast (not the real coastline); brightness = the two gauges' levels on their own scales, blended linearly. |
| Rain, snow, temperature, greenness, pressure, stored-water tints | Ian, monsoon, Africa, snow, Black Summer heat, Iberia heat | IMERG, MODIS MOD10CM, MODIS NDVI, GRACE-FO, MERRA-2 / ERA5 (model) | **Real** grids | Each real map is held for its whole step and only blends into the next in the last 15% of the step. Smooth between grid cells. Faded out organically at the edge of the downloaded area (no fade at the date line for global grids; patches stop 2 degrees short of the poles). Snow and greenness are clipped to the coastline. Greenness: colour ramp brown (NDVI 0.1 or less) to olive to deep green (0.9). Snow story temperature: blue tint only below 0 C plus a glowing 0 C line from the same grid. Africa rain drawn at 50% strength so the fires stay in front (stated). Iberia afternoon heat (GEOS-CF, model): warm-side tint on land only, clear at 20 deg C or less, then yellow, orange and deep red with opacity rising, both linear up to 44 deg C (stated). |
| NO2 fog (Cloud / Smoke / Tint) | Wuhan, Po Valley | NASA OMI OMNO2d | **Derived** | 15-day composites of clear-sky overpasses, small holes up to 1 degree filled, smoothed about 50 km, nothing drawn below 6x10^15 molecules/cm2. Po Valley haze density drawn 6x thicker than Wuhan's (still linear, stated) so it reads at that camera height, with a wider organic edge. The billow texture of Cloud mode and the puff positions of Smoke mode are illustrative; their density is the data. |
| Smoke particles | Canada smoke, Black Summer | GEOS-CF surface PM2.5 and 10 m wind (model) | **Derived** | Particles start at random places; each one's opacity is the model PM2.5 where it is, and it drifts on the model wind. The overall pattern is data; no single puff is a data point. |
| Wind streaks | Ian, Canada smoke, Po Valley, monsoon | ERA5 / GEOS-CF wind (model) | **Derived** | Streaks start at random places and are moved by the real wind grid; length = 3 hours of travel at that speed. Wind is interpolated between model times. |
| Hurricane track | Ian | IBTrACS best track | **Real** | 3-hourly positions; points between 6-hourly fixes are interpolated by IBTrACS itself (flagged in the data). Straight lines between points. |
| Hurricane spiral | Ian | IBTrACS wind | **Illustrative**, driven by data | Size fixed (about 420 km). Opacity and spin speed follow the best-track wind; the spiral shape is drawn. |
| Expected-front ring | Tonga | computed from the eruption time at 315 m/s | **Illustrative** (reference) | Dashed, labelled "expected front": shows where a wave at that speed should be; the gauges show what was measured. |
| Volcano marker + eruption flash | Tonga | USGS event us7000gc8r (place and time) | **Illustrative**, real place and time | A cone at the USGS position, about 350 km tall (not to scale); the flash starts at the real eruption time (04:14 UTC) and fades over a few story hours; its size and glow are illustrative. |
| Callouts | Planet Breathing, Black Summer | the story's own data | **Real** timing | Short notes pinned on the globe: the days the Mauna Loa CO2 swing is highest and lowest in 2021 (computed from the series), Canberra's 764 ug/m3 day. |
| Trees | Planet Breathing | MODIS NDVI cells | **Illustrative**, driven by data | One tree per greenness cell, jittered inside its cell; conifer or broadleaf by latitude and a random draw; height and leaf colour follow that cell's monthly greenness (bare brown at low greenness, deep green at high). |
| CO2 haze | Planet Breathing | NOAA Mauna Loa (one station) | **Illustrative**, driven by data | One global value drawn as a haze over the whole planet; it is not a map of CO2. |
| Ground (pixel grid) | start screen and global stories | NASA Blue Marble (GIBS); NDVI in Planet Breathing | Backdrop | Resampled into squares for the look; not story data. Off by default in regional close-ups and in the snow story (the smooth Blue Marble shows there); D toggles it. |
| Event sounds and brass | all | the stories' own event times and the hero signal | **Real** timing | Hits fire when the playhead crosses a real event; brass plays only where the hero is in the top 15% of its own range. |
