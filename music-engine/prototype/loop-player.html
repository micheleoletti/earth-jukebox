Follow knob: right = louder as intensity rises, left = fades out as intensity rises, middle = steady. Tone: low-pass filter. Settings are remembered in this browser.

Pick a rhythm above the knob (switching crossfades in time). Drag the knob up/down (or around), scroll on it, or use arrow keys. Keys 1–5 jump to a version, Space plays/stops. All 5 loops run in sync, so turning the knob only changes their volumes and never breaks the groove. Drums and synth layers are synthesized in the browser (synth-drums.js, loop-engine.js) and bounced to seamless loops. Optional drones: Spitfire LABS Textural Pads and Trumpet Fields (off in the default mix). See music-engine/README.md.
