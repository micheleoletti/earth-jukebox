Draw: click an empty cell. Move: drag a note (selected notes move together). Length: drag a note's right edge. Delete: right-click (or ⌃+click) a note, hold and drag to delete many, or Del on a selection. Select: ⇧/⌘+drag a box, ⇧+click to add, ⌘A all. Duplicate selection: ⌘B. Nudge: arrow keys. Undo: ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z.
Ghost notes (hollow, marked g): make selected notes ghosts with G or Ghost ⇄; the ghost vel knob sets how loud all ghost notes play, live. Rhythm: switch between rhythm sets; Bounce uses the selected one.
Velocity lane (bottom): drag up/down over any notes to set their velocity, no selection needed. Note brightness shows velocity. Rows: click a row name to hear it (left side = soft, right side = loud); right-click a row name to remove all its notes in this pattern. Each row is a group of 6 Thunder X3M keys played in turn (round robin). Space play/stop, 1–5 switch pattern.
Bounce to knob renders the selected rhythm's 5 intensities with the synthesized drums (a few seconds, Bounce all does every rhythm) and rebuilds the knob player's loops. Preview and bounce use the same synth, so what you hear here is what the knob plays.
